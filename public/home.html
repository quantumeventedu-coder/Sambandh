The Setu Journey

Eight stages, walked together.

After you match, Sambandh Setu gives you a shared path. A stage completes only when both of you complete it — nobody can move the other forward, and either of you can pause.

01

Connect

Find a relevant person and accept a connection.

02

Understand

Get to know each other through conversations at your own pace.

03

Reflect

Ten private questions. For every answer you choose: keep private, share, or share later.

04

Discuss

Talk through values, expectations and plans that matter to you both.

05

Agree

Note what you agree on, and what still needs time.

06

Family

Involve family when you are both ready — optional and consent-based.

07

Reflect again

Look back at how things have grown, privately and honestly.

08

Decision

Decide your next step together. No pressure, no timelines.

Different journeys. A shared tomorrow.

Lakshan

Honesty, with a person in the loop.

Lakshan notices when what someone says does not match what they do — and explains exactly why. It never decides that anyone is dishonest on its own.
